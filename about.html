How It All Started

A Humble Garage, One Vintage Drum Roaster, and a Dream

Roasted & Co. started with an obsession: coffee shouldn’t be a rushed commodity swallowed on the way to work. It should be a pause — a sensory journey that celebrates farmers, earth, and artisanal craft.

In 2014, our founder began micro-roasting green beans sourced directly from small high-altitude farms in Antioquia, Colombia and Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. Today, while our community has grown, our commitment remains untouched: roast fresh every morning, bake pastries from scratch, and treat every guest like family.

  • ✓ Direct farmer partnerships ensuring living wages and regenerative soil care.
  • ✓ Vintage cast-iron drum roasters calibrated for peak sweetness and body.
  • ✓ Zero artificial syrups or preservatives — all syrups made in-house.
Our Guiding Pillars

The Three Principles We Live By

Every bean we roast and pastry we bake is measured against our core philosophy.

Ethical Sourcing

We visit our partner farms annually. We pay up to 40% above fair-trade market rates to support sustainable living for harvesters.

Artisan Precision

Roasting is part art, part science. We track roast curves to the tenth of a degree to accentuate natural chocolate and fruit notes.

Community Haven

Our cafes are designed as quiet sanctuaries: plenty of power plugs, sunlit windows, soothing playlists, and warm smiles.
